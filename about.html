About Me

A little more about who I am and what drives me.

Who I Am

My name is Aliyu Idris, and I'm currently a 500 Level student in the Department of Computer Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.

I'm a person who loves to learn and stays curious about the world — how it works, how it came to be, and how technology keeps developing around us. I like understanding what makes things work, not just that they do — which is a big part of why I'm pointing my career toward Cybersecurity.

Hands-On Side

Alongside my coursework, I have a little practical knowledge of repairing circuits in electronic devices, along with small basic system repairs — both software and hardware. It's the same curiosity that draws me to cybersecurity: wanting to understand a system well enough to know where it might break, and how to fix or protect it.

What Drives Me

Technology is becoming the center of almost everything — and AI especially is reshaping how people work. My goal is to graduate, build a strong foundation in cybersecurity, keep learning tools like Linux and AI, and eventually start my own business while I continue growing my skills.
